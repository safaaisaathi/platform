## The contract surface

| Collection | Read | Write | The scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| `complaints` | ✅ | ❌ direct | The live staff ward scope |
| `complaintPhones` | ✅ district only | ❌ direct | **The raw complainant phone.** The DDO and the TDO only. An inspector sees `complaints.phoneMasked` |
| `wardDuty` | ✅ | ❌ direct | The duty board. **The server writes it.** The panel closes a ward through `markWardDutyCompleted` |
| `notices` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `drivers` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `staff` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `staffPhones` | ✅ district only | ❌ direct | All of them. **The raw phone's home** |
| `wards` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `routes` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `vans` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `trips` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |
| `ratings` | ✅ | ❌ direct | All of them |

**Every write is a callable. There is no direct write at all.**

## The seven calls it makes

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Sign in | `mintStaffToken({ staffId })` → a custom token with `role`, `staffId`, `wardIds` |
| Resolve a complaint | `setComplaintStatus({ complaintId, status, note?, resolvedPhotoUrl? })` |
| Send a notice | `publishNotice({ targetType, targetId, message })` |
| Edit a driver | `upsertDriver({ code, name, phone, vanId, routeId, wardIds, active })` |
| Manage staff | `upsertStaff({ staffId, name, role, wardIds, talukaId, phone, active })` |
| Set a depot | `setVanDepot({ vanId, lat, lng })` |
| Close a ward | `markWardDutyCompleted({ wardId, date? })` |

**The old `adminResolveComplaint` is gone.** It took `workerId` and `workerName` as client parameters, which means the client said who did the work, and the client can be edited. `setComplaintStatus` reads the staff member from the token and from the live staff document. CHG-0013.

## The two things the panel gets right that the other apps do not

### 1. Every write is a callable, so revocation is instant

**A revoked staff member loses access immediately, not after their token expires.** The callable reads the live `staff/{id}.active` on every privileged action.

**The cost.** Five functions and a stricter rule set. The panel's save buttons stop working if it writes directly, so this is a breaking change, tracked as `CHG-0004`.

### 2. The raw phone is at `staffPhones`, not on `drivers`

**The panel is the only consumer of the raw phone.** It reads `drivers.phoneMasked` for display and `staffPhones/{code}` for the actual number.

**Do not "helpfully" read `drivers.phone`.** It does not exist any more. The mask is `98XXXXXX10`.

## The scope refresh, and the one line the panel must show

**The staff claims are minted at sign-in.** A scope change lands at the next sign-in, within an hour.

**So the panel must show when the scope was last refreshed.** One line: *"your scope was last refreshed at 2026-11-01 09:12"*.

**Without it, a stale scope is invisible and the office blames the app.**
